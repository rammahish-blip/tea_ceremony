how to play
skip
tea flows clockwise
Tap any cup with tea inside.

Tea pours clockwise, one cup at a time.

The final drop determines what happens next.
collect across the gap
After the final drop lands, the game checks the next cup.

If that cup is empty, tea jumps across and serves the cup after — earning points.

If the next cup has tea, no collect happens — turn ends.

If both cups are empty, no collect either. Plan your final drop position carefully.
hidden tea cups
Some cups hide their tea amount.

Their value appears only when selected.

Plan carefully.
